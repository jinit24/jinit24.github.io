A few useful details.

Will Pluto capture every expense?

No. Your history depends on the records Pluto can successfully import. Some payments may be missing or need category corrections. Review the underlying transactions when using its insights.

Do I need to connect Gmail?

No. Gmail is an optional way to import receipts and payment alerts. You can explore planning features without connecting it. Imported spending analytics and reimbursement suggestions need transaction data to work from.

Can I import older transactions?

Yes. Historical backfill imports older Gmail receipts and payment alerts that Pluto recognises. Older history requires a subscription, and coverage depends on the messages available.

Does a reimbursement suggestion mean I've been paid?

No. Suggestions are based on your policies and transaction data. Review them and record the amount you actually received; a suggested amount is separate from confirmed reimbursement.
