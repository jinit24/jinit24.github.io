PLUTO — PRODUCT DEMO AND SCREENSHOTS
Prepared September 21, 2026

Pluto is a private expense tracker for iPhone and iPad with spending analytics,
reimbursement tracking, savings plans and historical backfill.

Website: https://jinit24.github.io/pluto/
App Store: https://apps.apple.com/in/app/pluto-spending-made-private/id6806493023
Media page: https://jinit24.github.io/pluto/media/

This is a slideshow composed from real app captures, not a live screen recording.
All financial figures are synthetic demonstration data, not customer results.
The reimbursement example shows an expense of INR 850, INR 420 confirmed received,
and INR 430 remaining personal cost. Suggestions alone do not count as repayment.
Savings suggestions are not guaranteed outcomes.

Current transaction import uses an optional read-only Gmail connection.
Coverage depends on receipts and payment alerts successfully recognised.
It is not a complete bank or cash-payment feed.

The first 30 days include the recent 30-day ledger without automatic charging.
Older history and continued access require a subscription. Buying starts the
paid term immediately; subscriptions renew unless cancelled. Prices vary by
storefront. Review the price and terms shown by Apple before purchasing.

When sharing, keep the demo-data labels and disclosures visible and include
the App Store link in the post. Do not describe these as customer testimonials,
guaranteed savings, complete payment coverage or a free-forever service.
