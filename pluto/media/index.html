What to know before trying it.

Pluto's current transaction import uses an optional read-only Gmail connection. Imported spending records stay on your device. Coverage depends on the receipts and payment alerts Pluto can successfully recognise; it is not a complete bank or cash-payment feed.

Reimbursement suggestions are not proof of payment. Confirm what you actually received. Read the reimbursement guide.
